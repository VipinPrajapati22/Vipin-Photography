Biography

Capturing the Unseen Architecture of Nature

"True magnification isn't just about optics €” it's about slowing down enough to perceive the quiet balance of natural ecosystems."

Macro Vipin is a nature photographer and educator based in India, specializing in extreme high-magnification macro photography, focus stacking, and atmospheric woodland landscapes.

With an eye for intricate biological form, his work documents the iridescent compound eyes of dragonflies, the fungal geometries of rainforest leaf litter, and the subtle interplay of mist in old-growth canopies.

Field Ethics Commitment

All subjects are photographed live in their wild natural habitat. No insects are ever refrigerated, sprayed, poisoned, or removed from their environment. Zero habitat disruption is the foundation of every image.
