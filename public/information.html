Biography

A Personal Journey in Nature Photography

"Photography is a way to slow down, explore nature, and notice small details."

Macro Vipin is a self-taught photography enthusiast based in India. Photography is a personal creative passion—an opportunity to spend time outdoors, explore local trails, and observe insects, plants, and natural light.

His work centers on nature macro close-ups, woodland paths, and mountain landscapes, capturing honest moments directly in the field.

Field Ethics

All subjects are photographed naturally in their wild environment without disturbing living creatures or damaging their habitat.
